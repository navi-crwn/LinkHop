Features

  • Lightning-Fast Shortening

    Custom memorable slugs or random IDs served instantly at hel.ink/l/{slug} with low-latency redirects.

  • Dynamic QR Codes

    Generate high-resolution SVG/PNG QR codes for print, packaging, and digital sharing.

  • Deep Click Analytics

    Track visitor countries (GeoIP), devices, browsers, operating systems, and referrers in real time.

  • Automated Threat Safety

    Proactive scanning via hop/safety and shared domain blacklists to safeguard your audience.

  • Granular Link Controls

    Set expiration dates, maximum click counts, and password protection for sensitive destinations.

  • Organization & Tags

    Group links into folders and tag campaigns for clean team management and aggregated reports.

  • HOP ID Single Sign-On

    Sign in once with your central HOP ID; no duplicate accounts or fragmented sessions.

  • Developer REST API

    Full API with scoped token authentication to programmatically create and monitor links.

Frequently asked questions

What happened to link-in-bio pages?

Bio pages have moved to BioHop (bio.hel.ink) as a dedicated, feature-rich app. Existing bio links redirect seamlessly.

Can I use custom slugs?

Yes, authenticated users can pick custom slugs for any link as long as they are available and allowed.

How does click tracking protect privacy?

Clicks are aggregated without storing raw personal data or selling user profiles, and logs are routinely pruned.

Is LinkHop free and open source?

Yes, LinkHop is released under the permissive MIT license for everyone to use and self-host.
